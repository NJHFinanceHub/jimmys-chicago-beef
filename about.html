Our Story

Two sisters, one truck,
named after our dad.

Why "Jimmy's"?

Jimmy is our dad — a Chicago guy with a big laugh and a standing order for the same Italian beef every Saturday. He's the reason we know what a real beef sandwich tastes like, and the reason this truck has his name on it.

We painted it sky blue, put the Chicago skyline on the side, and started serving the food we grew up on: Italian beef, Vienna dogs, Polish sausage, sport peppers, giardiniera. Simple stuff, done right.

Meet Loren & Shannon.

We're the Zukowski sisters — the two faces you'll see at the truck. We do the roasting, the prep, the grilling, the greeting, and the dishes. We answer the phone. We're proud of every beef that comes off this thing.

Small truck. High standards.

Everything is made in-house, start to finish:

  • Top round roasted low and slow — overnight, every night.
  • Vienna Beef franks and Polish sausages, shipped in from Chicago.
  • Giardiniera made in-house, so we can make it spicy.
  • Sturdy Italian rolls from a local Denver bakery — the only "local" shortcut we take, and it's a good one.
  • Sport peppers, yellow mustard, neon relish, tomato, pickle, celery salt. Never ketchup. Never.

Family Recipes

Every beef, every bun, every giardiniera jar — the way our dad taught us. No shortcuts, no compromises.
