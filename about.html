Our Story

A taste of family tradition.

Jimmy's Chicago Beef was built on family, faith, and a deep love for good food.

We are two sisters raised in Chicago, building our dream in Denver by bringing the bold flavors of our home to the mountains.

Named after our Dad, Jimmy's Chicago Beef is more than a food truck — it's a tribute to the man who taught us the value of hard work, dedication, and gathering around a great meal. When we were with our Dad, the kitchen was always the heart of every celebration.

We launched Jimmy's Chicago Beef in Denver with one mission: to serve authentic Chicago flavors done the right way. From slow-roasted beef and rich au jus to sweet peppers and spicy giardiniera, everything we serve is rooted in tradition and made with care and quality ingredients.

Every sandwich is made with intention. Every customer is treated like family.

From our family to yours — Welcome to Jimmy's.
