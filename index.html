Our Story

Named for our dad.
Built with our hands.

Jimmy was our father — a proud Chicago guy who loved a dipped Italian beef more than just about anything. When we lost him, we built this truck to keep him close. Same roast beef, same Vienna dogs, same sport peppers and neon relish he grew up on — served out of a sky-blue window in Denver.

We're Loren & Shannon Zukowski, Jimmy's daughters. We hope you're hungry.
